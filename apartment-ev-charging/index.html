The process

How the OPEX-based model works

Every community is different. We start by understanding your site, electrical capacity and likely charging demand, then assess whether the model is viable.

01

Site assessment

Review parking, available space, electrical connection, access, safety requirements and likely usage.

02

Investment & installation

If the site is suitable, agree scope, investment, approvals and responsibilities before installation.

03

Resident charging

Set up a suitable charging and payment process, including transparent usage measurement and tariffs.

04

Operations & maintenance

Define service, maintenance, electricity billing and operating responsibilities in the agreement.

Cars and two-wheelers

We can assess requirements for electric cars and two-wheelers. Charger types, quantities, power capacity and layout will be proposed only after reviewing the site and electrical feasibility.

What we need to assess your site

A suitable location and an appropriate electricity arrangement are starting points. Final feasibility depends on electrical capacity, approvals, safety, access, demand and the agreed commercial structure.

Nexura Dynamics will pay applicable electricity charges only as defined in the mutually agreed arrangement. Responsibilities for electricity supply, metering, tariffs, maintenance and any upgrades will be documented before work begins.
